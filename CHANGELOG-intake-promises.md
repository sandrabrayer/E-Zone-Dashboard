# Intake commitments on a phone call or WhatsApp conversation

Staff can select **משימות המשך לשיחה** in the existing contact form and record up to four
commitments in one save: send the catalogue, check missing documents, coordinate
a visit, or give a status update. Each gets its own chosen owner and due date. The ordinary
Tasks sheet supplies the card and My Day. The activity record keeps the original plan when
staff later complete, reschedule or reassign a task.

The catalogue task offers a copyable WhatsApp message from the card and My Day.
Staff attach the approved family edition in the existing business conversation.
After sending, they record the edition and recipient. The dashboard timestamps the
staff report and closes that task. Provider delivery receipts remain separate.

The existing contact submission identifier and Apps Script lock protect replays. Each
commitment has its own stable task identifier. All choices are validated before the first
write. A retry can finish an interrupted write, but a changed plan cannot add tasks to a
previously logged conversation. These actions record work for staff. They do not send
materials, accept clinical documents, schedule a visit, or change billing.

Acceptance covers the normal CRM form on a 360px phone and a second staff session on a
1280px desktop through the Express and Apps Script handlers with synthetic sheets.

The Maccabi documents board also prepares an individual on-screen request from the
current checklist. Received documents and those already in transit are omitted.
When there is nothing to request, the row explains whether any documents are still
on the way. Unrecognized or conflicting legacy lists require staff reconciliation.
The complete general reference remains available separately. Opening a draft
does not communicate with a family or change a document's status.
